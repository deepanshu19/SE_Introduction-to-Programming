Answer - A program is a set of instructions written in a programming language that tells a computer or device what tasks to perform. It helps users complete specific activities by processing data and producing the desired results. For example, when I use Instagram to upload a photo, the Instagram program follows instructions to add filters, save the image, and share it with my followers.

4.Write down the steps (as an algorithm, using simple numbered instructions) for ordering food on Swiggy — from opening the app to completing payment.

Answer - 
1. Start.
2. Open the Swiggy app.
3. Log in to your account if required.
4. Select or confirm your delivery location.
5. Search for a restaurant or food item.
6. Choose a restaurant from the list.
7. Browse the menu and select the desired food items.
8. Add the selected items to the cart.
9. Review the items in the cart.
10. Enter or confirm the delivery address.
11. Apply a coupon code if available.
12. Select a payment method.
13. Confirm the order details.
14. Complete the payment.
15. Receive order confirmation from Swiggy.
16. End.
